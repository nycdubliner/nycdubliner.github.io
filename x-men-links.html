ResurrXion Era (2017)

The "Back to Basics" era where the X-Men split into two primary teams: Gold (Veteran/Public) and Blue (Original Five/Secret).

GOLD
X-Men Gold (2017 - 2018) Led by Kitty Pryde. Return to the Mansion in Central Park.
BLUE
X-Men Blue (2017 - 2018) The time-displaced Original Five, mentored by Magneto.

The Jean Grey Resurrection (2018)

The return of the adult Jean Grey leads to a new third team focused on global advocacy and fighting the "hate machine."

RED
X-Men Red (2018 - 2019) Tom Taylor's run. Jean Grey leads a global team from Atlantis.

Modern Arakko Era (2022)

A completely different era, but the "Red" name returns for one of the greatest Storm stories ever told.

RED
X-Men Red (2022 - 2023) Al Ewing's masterpiece. The politics and war of Planet Arakko.
